# Product definition

## One sentence

Actually. is an obviously satirical, absurdly confident relationship historian
that listens to a mundane disagreement, preserves the boring factual record,
then produces an officious and wildly unhelpful comic ruling.

## The joke

Actually. heard the whole thing and still got it spectacularly wrong.

The contrast is the product:

- **Evidence Locker:** accurate, speaker-labelled transcript.
- **Actually’s reconstruction:** clearly invented comic interpretation.
- **Official finding:** disproportionate certainty about an insignificant event.
- **Reconsider the evidence:** a different verdict that denies ever changing.

## Primary flow

1. Both people acknowledge that recording is taking place.
2. The user starts listening. Audio stays on the device for up to 60 minutes.
3. If nothing happens, **Destroy the evidence** deletes it without an API call.
4. A disagreement occurs and someone presses **OBJECTION!**.
5. A 90-second closing-arguments window begins; either party can keep talking.
6. The timer expires or someone presses **Judge us now**.
7. Audio uploads once for transcription and is deleted locally after success.
8. Actually. shows sample quotations for each anonymous speaker label.
9. The users map those voices to names or leave comic generic labels.
10. The app generates the verdict, blame allocation, third culprit and treaty.
11. An appeal creates a contradictory verdict while denying any change.

## Tone

- British, dry, bureaucratic, theatrical and quotable.
- “Troll setting: maximum”, aimed at timing, wording, minor habits and household
  objects.
- Each human gets teased; neither is treated as a truthful oracle.
- One overlooked third culprit absorbs a meaningful share of blame.
- The final peace treaty is genuinely useful without becoming earnest.

## Guardrails that preserve the joke

These are product quality constraints, not generic disclaimers:

- Never present satire as fact.
- Never invent abuse, infidelity, crimes, diagnoses, addictions, threats,
  sexual conduct or other sensitive allegations.
- Never target protected characteristics, appearance, trauma, intelligence or
  vulnerabilities.
- Do not claim to detect lies or determine who is genuinely right.
- Keep exact transcript evidence visually separate from comic invention.

Crossing those boundaries makes the product less funny, less shareable and
materially riskier.

## Prototype scope

Included:

- Installable mobile-first web app
- 60-minute local recording limit
- Recoverable five-second device-local audio chunks
- Objection marker and 90-second closing window
- Speaker-labelled transcription
- Human speaker-name resolution
- Structured comic verdict
- Appeal, native sharing and Evidence Locker
- Built-in demo case

Not included yet:

- Accounts, cloud history or relationship profiles
- Background recording with the phone locked
- Screenshot or messaging-app ingestion
- Native Android/iOS applications
- Public multi-user billing, quotas or abuse controls
